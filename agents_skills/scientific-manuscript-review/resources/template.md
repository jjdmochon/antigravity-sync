# Manuscript Templates and Checklists

Use these structural templates and checklists when reviewing or drafting manuscript sections.

## Introduction Arc Funnel
1. **Context Statement:** Describe the broad medical or biological field and its general significance.
2. **Current Knowledge:** Outline existing work and what has already been established.
3. **Knowledge Gap:** Clearly and explicitly state the missing information (e.g., "However, the direct impact of X on Y remains unexplored").
4. **Hypothesis/Objective:** Conclude with the study's central purpose or research question.

## Results Paragraph Structure
- **Objective:** What specific question is being addressed?
- **Approach:** What assay, compound, concentration, or design was chosen?
- **Finding:** The quantitative results (including statistical values, standard deviations, and N-values).
- **Interpretation:** A concise summary of what this specific finding suggests.

## Scientific Clarity Checklist
- [ ] Are all statistical assertions paired with p-values, N-values, and error bars?
- [ ] Is vehicle/control treatment clearly identified for comparisons?
- [ ] Are there overstated claims that lack robust, direct experimental evidence?
