# Manuscript Review Methodology

This resource details the methodology for structural passes, logical checks, and narrative evaluation in scientific manuscripts.

## Logical Flow and Coherence
Check the overall structural organization against standard IMRaD format:
- **Introduction:** Funnels from general context to a specific knowledge gap, ending with a clear hypothesis.
- **Methods:** Fully reproducible details, clear statistical definitions.
- **Results:** Logically ordered experiments backed by data and statistics, with minimal editorializing.
- **Discussion:** Connects results back to the original hypothesis, compares findings to the literature, and addresses limitations transparently.

## Softening and Hedging Language
Ensure assertions correspond precisely to evidence strength:
- Use *proves* or *confirms* only if data is definitive (rare in exploratory life sciences).
- Prefer *suggests*, *indicates*, or *is consistent with* for correlation, n=1 datasets, or exploratory findings.
